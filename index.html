Process

A simple process for complex screens.

I use a practical design process that moves from understanding the problem to validating the interface before final handoff.

01

Research

Understand users, business goals, workflows, and technical constraints.

02

Structure

Define flows, information hierarchy, and low-fidelity wireframes.

03

Interface

Design polished screens with consistent spacing, typography, and components.

04

Prototype

Connect screens into clickable flows for review, feedback, and alignment.

05

Refine

Improve usability, prepare handoff, and iterate with developers and product teams.
